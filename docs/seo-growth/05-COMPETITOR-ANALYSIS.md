## 3. Gap analysis and differentiation

1. **B2B is open ground.** No competitor addresses distributors, foodservice or importers on a dedicated page. Family Eggs now does, in both languages. This is the clearest differentiator, and the reason the wholesale and international pages were prioritised.
2. **Trust signals are the biggest remaining gap.** Rahima and Al Jazira lead with certificates. If Family Eggs holds SFDA/MEWA licences, Saudi GAP, ISO 22000 or Halal certification, publishing them (with numbers) on a *Quality & licences* page is the highest-value content addition. **Nothing was added, because nothing is confirmed.**
3. **The spec data is already better than most.** Six grades, both pack formats, shelf life, temperature and humidity: more detailed than Al Watania, Rahima or Al Jazira. The next step is gram ranges per grade (as KUPCO does) and a one-page spec PDF, once the owner supplies the weights.
4. **Technical SEO is a relative strength.** Correct hreflang, a full JSON-LD graph, prerendered HTML and fast static delivery put the site ahead of several larger competitors, including AlGharbia's client-rendered site.
5. **Scale is not the battlefield.** Al Watania states 1.5 M eggs/day against Family Eggs' 430 k. Positioning should be *reliable, automated, family-run, direct from the farm*, not size.

Observed incidentally: the Arabian Farms and Golden Chicken homepages contained injected outbound links to unrelated or gambling sites on 2026-10-19. This was not investigated further.
